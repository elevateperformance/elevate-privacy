Delete your Elevate Performance account

Elevate Performance is developed by Andy Luis Amaro Borges.

How to request deletion

Email elevateperformanceapp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". Requests are processed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity
  • Your training plan, questionnaire answers, and coach chat history
  • Your training history, streak, and workout records

What is kept

Records of subscription purchases are retained where required for tax, accounting, and legal obligations. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play and must be cancelled separately in your Google Play account.
